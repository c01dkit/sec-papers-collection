More Sites

A curated list of useful resources for academic security research and conference tracking. Click any card to open in a new tab.

  • jeffhuang.comBest Paper Awards in Computer ScienceA long-running collection of best paper awards across 32 computer science conferences since 1996, maintained by Jeff Huang.↗
  • ccfddl.topCCF Conference DeadlinesTracks upcoming submission deadlines for conferences listed in the CCF recommended catalog, organized by topic and tier.↗
  • ccf.atom.imCCF Recommended Conferences and JournalsAn accessible mirror of the China Computer Federation's recommended catalog of international conferences and journals (Tier A/B/C).↗
  • connectedpapers.comConnected PapersVisualize related work as an interactive graph: enter a seed paper and explore prior work, derivative work, and similar papers in your field.↗
  • wispaper.aiWisPaperAI-powered literature search that takes natural-language queries and uses Intent Verification to surface the papers most relevant to your research goal.↗
  • cspapers.orgCS PapersA search engine for computer science conference papers (csrankings.org venues, including the security top tier) with fast filtering by venue/year and an open API.↗
